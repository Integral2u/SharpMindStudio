How pairing works

1. Create a pairing. 2. Scan the QR. 3. Enter PIN on the phone. 4. Approve here. 5. Phone opens Studio.

The phone only sees this PC’s address. Upstream credentials and the upstream address stay on this host.

Notes

Admin is loopback-only. Phone access needs `Studio:LanEnabled=true`. In-page phone camera scanning needs HTTPS; the system camera can scan the QR over HTTP.
